Path 03 · Joints and locomotionShow the single-joint → fit pair → creature dependencies

Before this path: use Chapter 02’s power habits and Chapter 03’s upload skills. Set the rover aside, remove its shield, and gather the separate servo supply and kit in Chapter 07.

  1. 07 · One actuatorMake the pointer repeatThree observed positions; clear motion; tested signal timeout.
  2. 08 · One pair firstFit two supported segmentsWheels roll; bridge fits; no collision through the small sweep.
  3. 08 · Then duplicateRun three bounded trialsFour segments, three joints, measured travel or a diagnosed constraint.

Finish line: the pointer is a completed mechanism. The crawler is an experiment; a wiggle alone does not establish locomotion.

⇢Designing a replacement bracket? Test the fit coupon, load path, and fastener interface before committing to the full part.
