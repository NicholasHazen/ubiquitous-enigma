Your revised lesson plan

Your route into robotics and model training.

You bring more than fifteen years of programming experience and an agentic development workflow. We’ll use that experience for instrumentation, interfaces, debugging, and reproducible experiments. The new skills are designing parts that fit, powering and measuring motion, and training a model whose results survive a fair evaluation.

Start at the bench: print and measure the small X1 Carbon fit strip. Keep the physical checkpoints even when the code is familiar. If parts are in transit, you can independently run the virtual joint; the model-training track does not require a working rover.
  1. 01 · FabricationMake a part fitDimensions, clearance, layer orientation, and a measured print.
  2. 02 · IntegrationMake motion dependablePower paths, torque, command timing, stop behavior, and repeatability.
  3. 03 · ExperimentEstablish a baselineA simulated joint, logged observations, and a 25-candidate comparison.
  4. 04 · Model-building planLearn to predict motionDataset → simple model → small neural network → held-out evaluation.

These arrows show the recommended reading order. The desktop branch can run on its own. Combining learned behavior with the real robot later requires both a checked physical mechanism and a model evaluated under the conditions it will encounter.

The reading sequence for your first model

Start with statistical learning and regression to give fitting and evaluation concrete meanings. Then work through the joint-data contract and simple predictors. Use PyTorch’s training workflow when you reach the small neural network, and finish with the locked evaluation. Read a section when its experiment needs it; you do not need to finish a textbook first.

Bring in mathematics as it becomes useful: vectors describe inputs and outputs; a mean and variance describe data; a derivative describes how a small parameter change affects a loss. The model lesson introduces those roles before asking you to use them. Reinforcement learning comes after you can train, evaluate, save, and reload a simpler model.

Use your coding agent without outsourcing the experiment

Give the agent a bounded implementation contract: input schema and units, allowed dependencies, expected artifacts, and the checks that must pass. Let it scaffold collectors, plots, and test harnesses. Keep the physical limits, dataset split, baseline, and evaluation criteria under your review.

Before a run, write a prediction and the evidence that would change your mind. Afterward, inspect actual traces and error cases. A plausible explanation from an agent does not establish that a model learned a useful relationship.
